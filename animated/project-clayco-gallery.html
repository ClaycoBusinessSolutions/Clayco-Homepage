Kali Hotel

Inglewood, California

The Kali hotel blends the iconic architecture and cultural heritage of Los Angeles with a modern twist, creating homage to the city’s storied past while embracing the innovation of the 21st century, representing glamour for the new generation.

Clayco is the builder of the project, and LJC will serve as the architect.

The fusion of old and new provides a fresh interpretation of LA’s unique identity, making it both timeless and forward-thinking.

Designed to create a unique skyline, the hotel’s cascading suites form a distinctive silhouette. The base opens up, serving as a gateway to Hollywood Park.

Guests can enjoy a range of luxury experiences, including a spa, pool deck, and rooftop lounge.
