Safety First

Work Safe. Live Safe. 24/7. We take a proactive approach to safety management to provide our clients with projects that go injury and incident free. Our own branded safety program, Clayco Safe, empowers all personnel on any job site to identify and correct hazards and at risk behaviors, reinforce safety compliance and execute the highest standard of safety procedures and practices.

Rise Together
